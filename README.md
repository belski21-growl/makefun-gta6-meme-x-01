A GTA 6 launch-week meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty ($500, top 50). The joke is the one every GTA fan lived: 13 years since GTA V, so launch day is a sick day. The picture shows a guy on his couch in Vice City at sunset, controller in hand, pizza and an energy drink on the table — and make.fun's ghost sitting next to him on the couch as player two, holding a sick note signed "Dr. Ghost" while texting the boss ("morning boss, can't come in today"). The wall calendar is crossed off day by day, 4,811 days of waiting. Caption in the picture: "TOOK A SICK DAY FOR GTA 6 / WAITED 4,811 DAYS IN VICE CITY / MY GHOST AGENT GOT PAID ON make.fun". See `fix-log.md` for the Goldfish-review fix round (Nov 2026).

The post itself is the entry: it goes up on X from @CharleJP with the meme attached, carrying the bounty link, @makefunai, and the play code `makefun-lanyard-glove-velvet`. `POST.json` names the post; `main.ts` copies it to the output for the gate. See `build-log.md` for the suggested post text and the steps still open before submit.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
